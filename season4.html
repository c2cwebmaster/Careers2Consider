Nicole Grob

Environmental Scientist

Nicole Grob is passionate about protecting our rivers and lakes. Ms. Grob has a bachelor's degree in fisheries and aquatic sciences as well as a wetland delineation certification. She has worked for both the Utah and Colorado state departments of natural resources as a fisheries technician. She later went on to be a lab manager at Texas State University, working with freshwater mussels, and is now an environmental scientist for a Texas river authority.

Dr. Vaughn Brinegar

Optometrist

Dr. Vaughn Brinegar is a Therapeutic Optometrist (Optometric Glaucoma Specialist) and has been providing comprehensive eye care since 2003. She is dedicated to helping patients achieve their best possible vision through advanced clinical care and the latest treatment options. She has extensive experience in the diagnosis and management of glaucoma and other ocular diseases, with a special interest in specialty contact lens fitting for patients with complex visual needs.

Dr. Brinegar is highly experienced in fitting scleral lenses for patients with keratoconus, irregular corneas, and post-surgical corneas, helping many individuals achieve comfortable vision when traditional contact lenses are no longer an option. She also fits a wide range of specialty contact lenses tailored to each patient’s unique needs.

Dr. Brinegar believes in combining compassionate patient care with the latest advances in eye care technology to provide personalized treatment plans for patients of all ages.

Vrajesh Karkhanis

Cancer Researcher

Vrajesh Karkhanis is a scientist and Data Manager at Baylor College of Medicine/Texas Children’s Hospital.

Stephen Gauvain

NASA Crew Trainer

Stephen Gauvain is a Professional Technical Individual providing 14 years of experience in Project Management of multi-disciplinary engineering tasks, Instructing, Real-Time Flight Operations, and Product Development for NASA. He simultaneously carried out two other job positions in addition to his primary task. Gauvain achieved a Silver Snoopy award, given to less than 1% of the NASA workforce for outstanding work achievements. He subscribes to the foundations of NASA’s Mission Operations: Discipline, Competence, Confidence, Responsibility, Toughness, Teamwork, and Vigilance.

Dr. Ashima Aggarwal

Speech Pathologist

With over eight years of experience as a Speech-Language Pathologist (SLP), Dr. Ashima Aggarwal is dedicated to helping students develop the communication skills they need to succeed academically, socially, and beyond. She earned her Ph.D. in Linguistics from the University of Florida and her Master's degree in Speech-Language Pathology from The Ohio State University, bringing together a strong foundation in both the science of language and evidence-based clinical practice.

Dr. Aggarwal has experience providing speech and language services to K–12 students with diverse communication needs in school settings. She collaborates with educators, families, and multidisciplinary teams to deliver individualized, evidence-based therapy that supports each student's communication and academic success. Dr. Aggarwal is committed to creating inclusive, supportive environments where every child has the opportunity to communicate effectively and thrive.
