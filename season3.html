Carmen Wong Fisch

Portrait Photographer

I help leaders, founders, and professionals look as confident on camera as they are in life. My portraits aren’t only about posing — they’re about presence. Through calm direction, flattering light, and genuine connection, I reveal the quiet confidence that’s already there. Based in Basel, I work with clients across Europe who want portraits that reflect authenticity, strength, and ease. This is The Art of Being Seen. Many of my clients are accomplished professionals — CEOs, founders, and executives who’ve built remarkable careers, yet rarely paused to be truly seen. Some have been too busy leading. Others have simply avoided the camera altogether. That’s where I come in. In my private Basel portrait studio near the Novartis Campus, I create space for calm and connection — a guided experience that helps even the most camera-shy feel at ease. With a background in modeling and years behind the lens, I understand how vulnerable it can feel to be photographed. My approach is intuitive and grounded in empathy, drawing on light, body language, and quiet direction to bring out your natural confidence. You’ll leave with portraits that look like you — just seen through softer light.

Cheryl Magnuson

Interior Designer

Cheryl Magnuson is the owner of Majestic Interior Design, located in Bloomington, Illinois, since opening her business in 2006. She is a member of Bloomington-Normal Sunrise Rotary and she serves on the membership committee for that organization. She is also an alumni of the class of 2014 Multi-Cultural Leadership Program, and she thoroughly enjoys being involved in the community. Cheryl serves on CEO Council, a division of the Chamber of Commerce, as a Director on the Normal Public Library Foundation board, the Illinois Shakespeare Festival, and the Convention and Visitor’s Bureau Board of Directors for the city of Bloomington. She also participates in committee events for Easter Seals, Red Cross, OSF, and Miller Park Zoo. In her spare time, Cheryl enjoys spending time with her friends, visiting wineries, and traveling the globe!

Loveleen Aulakh

Mixed Media Artist

Lovleen Aulakh is an Austin based artist who creates unique mixed media paintings with inks, acrylics, 3D textures, gilding and mirror work. Her meditative art themes range from abstract to spiritual. Lovleen’s work is displayed and sold at several galleries and art centers in and around Austin. Even though she is a trained architect who worked over a decade in the sustainable design and construction industry, art has always been her calling. She got her first art commission in high school and has been creating as well as teaching art in her leisure time ever since. During 2020 social distancing time she got uninterrupted time to paint and officially started Inspired Art Studio in 2022. You can explore her work at www.Lovleenart.com

Rakhi Mutreja

Upcycle Artist/Entrepreneur

Rakhi Mutreja is the founder and creative force behind The Spinning Wheel ATX, a unique studio in Austin that blends sustainable recycling craft with the timeless art of pottery. Passionate about environmental consciousness and hands-on creativity, Rakhi has built a space where discarded materials are transformed into meaningful art and clay becomes a medium for expression and connection. With a deep belief that creativity should be both joyful and purposeful, she inspires children and adults alike to explore, experiment, and create with intention. Through workshops, community events, and immersive studio experiences, Rakhi continues to foster a vibrant artistic community where sustainability and imagination beautifully intersect.

Raman Nimmala

Filmmaker

Raman Nimmala is a filmmaker exploring stories at the intersection of plural cultural and social identities. He tells them with heart, humor, and, when possible, cheap thrills. He holds an MFA in Film from Columbia University and is based between New York and India. Raman’s directorial debut, O’Sey Balamma, world premiered at the Sundance Film Festival 2026. This film marks the first-ever Telugu language film to premiere at the festival. Raman has produced multiple films that have screened at festivals such as HollyShorts, Frameline, New Orleans Film Festival and beyond. His short Rainbow Girls won the Grand Jury Award at NewFest and was selected as one of five films to represent Columbia University’s Film Program in New York and Los Angeles. Raman is a recipient of the Arthur J. Krim Memorial Scholarship Award and has worked in theatrical distribution and marketing at Variance Films. He is the co-founder of Andarated Productions. Prior to filmmaking, Raman worked in Finance in New York City.

Myrna Lewis

Industrial Designer

Myrna Lewis is an industrial designer with almost 10 years of experience in the appliance, enterprise electronics, and now primarily outdoors space. She currently works as an industrial designer at Trailside Creative, specializing in soft goods bag and backpack design, but has worked at design consultancies for the last 7 years and therefore carries a multidisciplinary design background; including enterprise electronics, some UX design and other consumer/enterprise products. She values the way design shapes the way people see things, as well as empowers them to imagine, create, and implement the future.

Erika Huey

Voice Artist

Erika Huey (also known as Erika Huey Shiun Tan) is a versatile Malaysian-born actress, multilingual voiceover artist and model who has established a significant presence in both the Southeast Asian and European entertainment sectors. Recognized as one of the Top 10 Malaysia Voice Artists for 2025 and a nominee for the 2026 SOVAS (Society of Voice Arts and Sciences) awards, Erika is a linguistic powerhouse, fluent in English, Mandarin, and Malay, with professional proficiency in French. Her acting portfolio includes recurring roles in popular Singaporean series like C.L.I.F. 5, Crescendo, and China’s iQIYI production The Ferryman: Legends of Nanyang, as well as lead performances in award-winning international short films. Currently based in Europe and represented by DBA Agency in London, Erika continues to push the boundaries of storytelling through her unique blend of cultural versatility, professional modeling experience, and performative depth.
