Terry Szuplat

Presidential Speechwriter

Terry Szuplat is an award-winning author, speaker, and trainer whose keynote presentations and workshops empower audiences with the communications skills he learned as one of President Barack Obama's longest-serving White House speechwriters. He crafted nearly 500 speeches on critical global issues and traveled to over 40 countries. His book, "Say It Well," became a bestseller, teaching people how to speak clearly and confidently. Today, Terry runs his own firm, Global Voices Communications, helping leaders in politics, business, NGOs, philanthropy, and entertainment inspire audiences around the world. As a keynote speaker and trainer, he takes audiences on a journey behind the scenes of the White House and shares the skills than can help anyone become a better speaker, communicator, and leader. He also teaches political speechwriting as an Adjunct Professor at American University's School of Public Affairs.

Anuveer Gill

Technical Support Engineer

As a Technical Solutions Engineer at Google, I specialize in supporting Workspace products, with a primary focus on ChromeOS and the Chrome Browser. My team and I partner with education and enterprise customers, acting as their trusted advisors to resolve complex technical challenges. This involves deep troubleshooting, root cause analysis, and the development of tailored solutions. Furthermore, we collaborate closely with product engineering teams, advocating for customer needs and providing valuable feedback to drive continuous improvement in product features, reliability, and user experience. This direct engagement with engineering ensures our products remain robust, intuitive, and effectively address the evolving needs of our customers.

Swapna Dutt

Film Producer

Swapna Dutt Chalasani is a highly influential Telugu film producer, playing a pivotal role in the evolution of modern Indian cinema. Inheriting a rich heritage from her father, C. Ashwini Dutt, she began her career within Vyjayanthi Movies, gaining invaluable experience before establishing her own production house, Swapna Cinema, alongside her sister, Priyanka Dutt. Her career is characterized by strategic growth and a commitment to producing high-quality content. Notably, she has overseen the production of numerous successful films, including the award-winning biographical drama "Mahanati," which garnered her a National Film Award, and the widely acclaimed romantic drama "Sita Ramam." Her strategic vision extends beyond feature films, as she has also ventured into web series and television production, showcasing her adaptability and business acumen. She actively participates in all aspects of production, from script selection and casting to marketing and distribution, demonstrating a comprehensive understanding of the film industry. Her ability to identify and support compelling narratives has solidified her reputation as a key figure in Telugu cinema, contributing significantly to its commercial and artistic success.

Arpita Judy

Dentist

Dr. Arpita Judy DDS provides both general and cosmetic dentistry in the Austin area. She takes great pride in her mission to care for her patients and in her sheer passion for general and cosmetic dentistry. She describes herself as being every bit as passionate about dentistry today as she was during her first day on the job. Dr. Judy completed her undergraduate studies at the University of California, Irvine, and due to her outstanding performance and achievements there she was accepted into dental school a year early at the prestigious University of Southern California at the young age of 20. She graduated with high honors and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery in 1998. Dr. Judy went on to complete her post-graduate General Practice Residency in 1999 at the Dallas VA Hospital. She carries a number of certifications related to general and cosmetic dentistry, including Oral Conscious Sedation, Nitrous Oxide Sedation, usage of the SiroLaser, CEREC one-day dentistry, and Invisalign.

Terence Perdon

Middle School Math Teacher

Terence Perdon is a Math Teacher with more than 15 years of teaching experience in the Round Rock Independent School District. With a Bachelor's Degree in Education from the University of Texas at Austin, Terence is certified to teach Math at the middle school and high school level and has been responsible for educating more than 2000 students in his career. He is fully committed to creating a fun, safe and engaging learning environment and has dedicated his career as a teacher to show students a real path to success as well as help them reach their full potential.

Al Agrawal

Cybersecurity Professional

Al Agarwal is a sales expert who knows how to handle every step of selling, from creating proposals to making deals and keeping customers happy. He's worked with big internet and tech companies. He's also really good at building partnerships with other companies, especially when it comes to the technical side of things. Mr. Agarwal helped companies sell their products and has a lot of experience helping customers before and after a sale. In fact, he helped one big partner go from no sales to selling 5 million dollars worth of products! He's worked in lots of different areas, like sales, customer service, marketing, and helping businesses grow. He understands things like how to look closely at network traffic, find unusual activity, stop attacks, and keep networks running smoothly.

Seshagiri Rao Vellanki

Aerospace Scientist

Dr. Seshagiri Rao Vellanki is an Indian scientist and is former distinguished scientist and associate director of the Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC), Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). He worked in ISRO for more than 39 years in various capacities. He also served in ISRO as Prof. Satish Dhawan for three years. He was instrumental in setting up state of the art Real Time Systems and computer networks for flight safety at SDSC SHAR. He also developed data processing software required for displaying flight trajectories.

Suchita Rao

Educator/Principal

Mrs. Suchita Rao is a dynamic and accomplished educationist with a strong history of leadership and innovation. Serving as the Director of Academics and Principal at BLOSSOMS INTERNATIONAL since 2010, she has been instrumental in shaping the academic direction of a network of schools and training institutes. Her expertise has been acknowledged through the Education Excellence Award 2025 and numerous state-level awards. Mrs. Rao is also a published author, creating valuable resources for children and aspiring teachers. Her career reflects a deep commitment to modern, value-driven education, the mentorship of women professionals, and providing guidance to students and families. Additionally, she has actively contributed to the well-being of underprivileged communities.

Shaila Abdullah

Graphic Designer/Author

Shaila Abdullah is a designer and author based in Austin, Texas, and the founder of My House of Design, a graphic design and communication agency. As a certified small business, she collaborates with federal, state, and local government entities, as well as educational institutions, universities, and nonprofits to deliver communication and design solutions. Shaila has authored six books, including Saffron Dreams, Beyond the Cayenne Wall, The Doll Whisperer, My Friend Suhana, Rani in Search of a Rainbow, and A Manual for Marco.
